About D.Y.C Academy

Building footballers. Shaping character. Growing a community.

Who We Are

D.Y.C Academy is a youth football development platform focused on nurturing players through quality training, teamwork, discipline and competitive opportunities.

Our Vision

To create a strong pathway where young footballers can grow their ability, character and confidence and pursue opportunities in football and beyond.

Our Mission

To provide consistent coaching, player development, match exposure and a positive environment for boys and girls.

Our Values

Discipline • Respect • Teamwork • Hard Work • Integrity • Growth

D.Y.C ACADEMY

Developing football talent through discipline, teamwork, education and opportunity.
